Our Story

Bringing a vibrant Izakaya experience designed especially for the Filipino and Vietnamese communities of San Diego.

The Concept

Mira Mesa is full of incredibly diverse and rich cultures. Yet, we felt there was a need for a late-night, intimate gathering spot—a place where people of Filipino and Vietnamese heritage could enjoy their favorite comfort flavors alongside classic Japanese tapas, paired with ice-cold beer and sake.

Thus, Izakaya Raki was born. We created an environment filled with earthy wood tones, reminiscent of a peaceful forest retreat, allowing you to wind down after a long day.
